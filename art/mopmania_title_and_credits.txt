 MOP MANIA ULTRA DELUXE - TITLE SCREEN AND CREDITS (first pass)
 80 columns x 30 rows. Play each sequence in order: clear the
 screen, print the frame, wait the listed hold time. The title's
 last 4 frames are an idle loop until the player presses Enter.
 The credits roll can also be done by printing CREDITS one line
 at a time with a short delay.
